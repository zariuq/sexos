Integration policy
------------------

Incorporate upstream edits. Retain an alternative when its behavior is an
improvement within the same architecture and independent checks establish
the difference. Preserve GLOBAL_VAS addressing, BOOT-only capability genesis,
typed PDX authority and explicit hardware enforcement boundaries. An address,
domain number or task number is not itself a grant.

Addressability, authority and enforcement remain distinct: GLOBAL_VAS names
memory, typed PDX decides access, and PKU enforces the sanctioned boundary.
Keep the freestanding, non-POSIX service model. Preserve revision identity
through snapshot, transform, swap and commit as that engine is implemented.
Language choice does not substitute for this discipline: the Cesta port must
preserve explicit memory custody, authority and hardware protection across
separate server binaries as well as within checked functions.

The corrected bounded-history ordering in sexstore remains useful: upstream
has not changed that component's source. Its current ABI and shared-library
dependencies nevertheless require review. Other alternatives, including the
correlated RPC service and stricter kernel enforcement profile, need explicit
comparison with the updated contracts before being counted as integrated.

Certificates, caches and interrupted work
---------------------------------------

A cached policy result must retain the caller's actual domain/task identity,
the exact grant identity and validity stamp (including generation where defined),
requested resource/rights, and every state identity or revision on which the
conclusion depends. Revocation, reuse or relevant mutation must
invalidate the corresponding conclusion. A generation field's presence alone
does not establish that lookup or revocation checks it. The authoritative PDX
invocation must still validate held authority before an effect; an estimate or
probabilistic belief cannot authorize that effect.

Carry distinct outcomes for incomplete, retryable, exhausted and quiescent
work. Failure to obtain a published scheduler head is not by itself a proof of
quiescence. Preserve residual work and wake obligations across interruption.

For each representation change, state the preserved observation: permissions,
protocol order, values, effects, cost bounds or another declared property. Admit the
actual plans and keep independent execution/memory/boot controls. Agreement
with a checker's own transition rules is not automatically a theorem about the
intended external property. Readable-C binary identity is a separate guarantee.

Current reply bookkeeping uses the indexed64-bit registry domain counters.
Exhaustion refuses before enqueue; every accepted call keeps a consumable right.
A failed enqueue returns exactly its reservation. Fault-notice publication uses
the same admission rule and cancels prepared metadata on refusal. Repeated
completion and frame reuse keep their separate exact transaction identities.
The current BOOT also checks actual captured tasks and held lifecycle, close,
route, self and activation grants against the catalog before any user entry.
The expanded catalog now includes sixteen finite Quil, nine finite Spindle and
three Browser incarnations, twenty-eight app entries and forty domain records. Sext's lifecycle lookup and pending
arrays share the catalog-derived bound; the tail must not retain a literal
six-entry limit. Typed entropy, clock and trust source grants are described in
SOURCES.txt. PCI command/configuration is implemented with separate component/CPU gates
(PCI.txt). The held history roster/query and paired NVMe history recovery join
are implemented (HISTORY.txt). Retirement/workspace and CPU activation
are implemented with their own qualification scopes (RETIREMENT.txt).

Proposed upstream change
------------------------

The sexcos branch adds exact complex values to Spindle's calc on top of the
pinned source: a number with an i suffix is imaginary, re, im and conj take a
value apart, norm and abs measure it, interfere(a, b) is
|a+b|^2 - |a|^2 - |b|^2, and whole complex results show in hex, bin and oct.
The Cesta calculator follows that branch. tests/spindle_calc_current.py reads
the branch's calc_command.rs by the blob and SHA-256 recorded in
interfaces/spindle_calc_current.source.json, checks that it gives the pinned
answer on generated inputs without the new syntax, and compares the checked
Cesta unit with it. A pin that includes the change replaces the branch.
